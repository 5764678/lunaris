# Vendored copy of video-shotcraft

This is a copy of the `video-shotcraft` skill (Apache License 2.0, see `LICENSE`), taken from the
owner's synced Claude plugins on 22 November 2026 for the LUNARIS video session, which could not
load the plugin. It lives only on the branch `claude/video-shotcraft-skill` and is not merged.

All audio files (`*.mp3`, `*.wav`, `*.m4a`) are left out: they are third-party Mixkit assets
whose licences do not allow redistribution as files. `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md` is kept so
music and sound-effect cues can be named. The TD mixes the actual audio at assembly, from a
local copy.
