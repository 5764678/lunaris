# Thai Basic Education Curriculum Indicators that LUNARIS Can Map To (ม.1–ม.6, with ป.1–ป.6 context)

Scope and sourcing notes, read first:
- The operative curriculum for secondary schools in Nov 2026 is still หลักสูตรแกนกลางการศึกษาขั้นพื้นฐาน พุทธศักราช 2551 (ฉบับปรับปรุง พ.ศ. 2560). The new competency-based curricula cover only early childhood and primary (see Key Question 6).
- Primary text source for the indicators: an OBEC document by สำนักวิชาการและมาตรฐานการศึกษา สพฐ. titled "ตัวชี้วัดระหว่างทางและตัวชี้วัดปลายทาง ตามหลักสูตรแกนกลางการศึกษาขั้นพื้นฐาน พุทธศักราช ๒๕๕๑ กลุ่มสาระการเรียนรู้วิทยาศาสตร์และเทคโนโลยี (ฉบับปรับปรุง พ.ศ. 2560)". It is hosted on Google Docs, not on obec.go.th. I extracted it with pdftotext and checked its ม.3 strand-3 wording against an independent DLTV unit plan, which matches word for word. The Thai below is normalized: I fixed PDF-extraction artefacts such as split vowel and tone marks ("ทีป่ รากฏ" → "ที่ปรากฏ") and line breaks. I did not change any wording.
- I could not open IPST's own strand-3 standards PDF or the printed OBEC indicators book. A copy of the book, "พิมพ์ครั้งที่ 1 พ.ศ. 2560, สพฐ.", appears on anyflip.com in search results but was not opened. IPST's official PDF for standard ว 2.3 (https://www.ipst.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PhysicalSci_Standard_2_3_Curriculum2560.pdf) confirms one thing: upper-secondary basic indicators carry grade-specific codes (e.g. "ม.5"). This is consistent with the OBEC document, which places biology at ม.4, physical science at ม.5, and Earth and space science at ม.6.
- I found no evidence that the strand-3, ว 4.x or math indicator texts were amended after 2560. OBEC later split indicators into "ระหว่างทาง" (interim/en-route) and "ปลายทาง" (terminal/end-point). That is an implementation aid; the indicator wording is unchanged.

## 1. Science strand 3 (วิทยาศาสตร์โลก และอวกาศ): exact standards and indicators on the Moon, phases, eclipses, tides, Sun–Earth–Moon, day/night, seasons and space technology

### Takeaway
Lower secondary has Moon, Sun and space-technology indicators in only one grade, ม.3: ว 3.1 ม.3/1–ม.3/4 (orbits by gravity, seasons and the Sun's apparent motion, Moon phases, moonrise/moonset timing and tides, and space technology/exploration). ม.1 and ม.2 have no ว 3.1 indicators; their strand-3 indicators are atmosphere, weather and energy (ว 3.2). Eclipses (ป.6), day/night (ป.3), Moon phases (ป.4) and space technology (ป.6) are first taught in primary. They come back at ม.3, and at ม.6 for space exploration.

### Inferences
- How LUNARIS features map to the ม.3 indicators:
  - The SPICE-lit globe, terminator and sub-solar point fit ว 3.1 ม.3/2 (apparent solar motion; incidence-angle reasoning) and ม.3/3 (phases emerge from Sun–Moon–Earth geometry).
  - The "Earth visibility windows" and sky charts support the moonrise/moonset-timing part of ม.3/3. They show it in reverse, as Earth's apparent motion seen from the Moon, so teachers need to bridge back to the Earth view.
  - The real NASA mission data (LOLA, SPICE) and the site-selection task fit ม.3/4 (space technology and "progress of space-exploration programmes, from collected data").
  - LUNARIS does not cover tides (the other half of ม.3/3).
- ม.3/2 and ม.3/3 use the verb "สร้างแบบจำลอง" (construct a model). Judges may expect students to build or manipulate a model, not only watch a simulation. LUNARIS lessons should therefore have students predict, set parameters and explain, and should include a quick physical-model check (lamp and ball) so the claim of meeting "สร้างแบบจำลอง" holds.
- At ม.6, LUNARIS supports ม.6/10 (space exploration with spacecraft and data) and ม.6/8 (habitability, here "conditions for a crewed lunar outpost"). Its illumination analysis is a lunar analogue of ว 3.2 ม.6/7 (solar energy received varies with latitude and incidence angle; at the lunar pole, near-horizon Sun gives extreme illumination asymmetry).
- Eclipses sit only at ป.6, and day/night first at ป.3. LUNARIS can revisit them at ม.3 as prior knowledge (e.g. lunar day/night length of about 29.5 Earth days) but cannot claim a secondary eclipse indicator.

## 2. Upper secondary (ม.4–ม.6): learning outcomes for the Earth–astronomy–space additional courses and the basic course on the Moon, celestial sphere and coordinates, Sun position, space exploration and space technology

### Takeaway
IPST's additional course "โลก ดาราศาสตร์ และอวกาศ" runs as 6 volumes, one per semester across ม.4–ม.6. Astronomy is all in ม.6:
- เล่ม 5: universe, stars, solar system, and space technology (บทที่ 16).
- เล่ม 6: บทที่ 17 ทรงกลมฟ้า, with horizon and equatorial coordinates, Sun and star rising/setting paths and solar time, and บทที่ 18 on planetary apparent positions.

Its learning outcomes on the celestial sphere and horizon coordinate system (altitude/azimuth) match LUNARIS's horizon sky charts very closely. No upper-secondary outcome is specifically about the Moon.

### Inferences
- LUNARIS's sky chart ("Sun and Earth paths over the local terrain horizon") is an altitude/azimuth horizon-coordinate display. It maps almost one-to-one onto บทที่ 17 activities 17.1–17.3 and 17.6 and onto outcomes 1–3. It adds a twist: the observer stands on the Moon, so students must transfer the horizon-coordinate concept to a new body. That is a strong far-transfer and higher-order-thinking claim.
- Outcome 3 (apparent solar time from the Sun's meridian transits) suggests a LUNARIS extension: let students time "local lunar noon" at a site over a lunation. This would be a direct analogue.
- The site-selection task maps to ว 3.1 ม.6/10 / outcome 6 (space exploration and applications "ในอนาคต") and could be offered as the "นำเสนอแนวคิด" product.
- The additional course reaches only science-programme (สายวิทย์) students. The basic ม.6 indicators reach everyone, but the ม.6 basic course is short (about 20 hrs per school example).

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve verbatim learning outcomes for ม.6 เล่ม 5 (บทที่ 13–16). The outcome for บทที่ 16 is presumably the space-exploration outcome identical to ว 3.1 ม.6/10, but this is unverified.
- The ว30266 outcomes come from a school document that mirrors IPST. I did not see IPST's master list of outcomes for the whole additional course (one search snippet mentioned "รวม 19 ผลการเรียนรู้" without a verifiable source).
- Grade placement of the additional course is a school decision; IPST's labelling is ม.4 เล่ม 1–2, ม.5 เล่ม 3–4, ม.6 เล่ม 5–6.

## 3. สาระที่ 4 เทคโนโลยี: computing science (ว 4.2) and design and technology (ว 4.1) indicators for ม.1–ม.6 that a data-driven site-selection activity could satisfy

### Takeaway
The strongest technology matches are:
- ว 4.2 ม.3/2: collect, process, evaluate and present data.
- ว 4.2 ม.3/3: evaluate the credibility of data. This matches LUNARIS's evidence and provenance labels.
- ว 4.2 ม.3/1: develop an application integrated with another subject.
- ว 4.2 ม.5/1: collect and analyse data and use computing knowledge to solve real problems.
- The ว 4.1 engineering-design chain, especially ว 4.1 ม.3/3 and ม.4/3: design a solution by analysing, comparing and deciding under constraints and resources, i.e. trade-off decisions.

## 6. Status (late 2026) of the competency-based curriculum and current MOE/OBEC policy; how LUNARIS aligns

### Takeaway
As of Oct–Nov 2026 the competency-based curriculum is being phased in, primary only:
- 2568 (2025): early childhood and ป.1–3, about 4,440 schools.
- 2569 (2026): adds ป.4–6, 4,221 schools.
- 2570 (2027): planned to add lower and upper secondary, but the secondary framework "อยู่ระหว่างจัดทำ" (is still being drafted).
- No nationwide mandate has been set; MOE will evaluate over 2568–2570.

For ม.1–ม.6 the 2551 (rev. 2560) indicators therefore remain the operative frame. LUNARIS should claim indicator alignment now, and position itself as ready for the competency-based secondary pilot from academic year 2570, the same year as the award.

## 7. Recommended best-fit grade level(s)

### Takeaway
Target ม.3 as the primary grade. It is the only secondary grade where Moon phases, the Sun's apparent motion, seasons and space technology are core indicators for all students (ว 3.1 ม.3/2–ม.3/4). The same grade also carries matching computing (ว 4.2 ม.3/1–ม.3/3), engineering design (ว 4.1 ม.3/2–ม.3/4) and math (ค 2.2 ม.3/2 trig, ค 3.1 ม.3/1 statistics) indicators, about 11–12 mappable indicators in one grade. Offer a ม.6 extension for science-programme classes: additional เล่ม 6 บทที่ 17 ทรงกลมฟ้า (horizon coordinates, Sun paths, solar time), เล่ม 5 บทที่ 16 space technology, and basic ว 3.1 ม.6/8–ม.6/10 with ค 3.1 ม.6/1.

### Cited Findings
- ม.3 indicator set: ว 3.1 ม.3/1–ม.3/4; ว 4.1 ม.3/1–ม.3/5; ว 4.2 ม.3/1–ม.3/4. — [OBEC doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WHgfLEJniuNNSfvbX9VaEOmDaOlwqlOG/export?format=pdf). Math ค 2.2 ม.3/2, ค 3.1 ม.3/1. — [OBEC math doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1md7-JRZlSwKme8-BrMW6jSaCRVN6tBA3/export?format=pdf)
- ม.1 and ม.2 contain no ว 3.1 (astronomy and space) indicators. — [OBEC doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WHgfLEJniuNNSfvbX9VaEOmDaOlwqlOG/export?format=pdf)
- ม.6 astronomy, celestial sphere and space-technology content: basic ว 3.1 ม.6/1–ม.6/10 ([OBEC doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WHgfLEJniuNNSfvbX9VaEOmDaOlwqlOG/export?format=pdf)) and additional เล่ม 5–6 ([IPST proj14](https://proj14.ipst.ac.th/m4-6-earth/)).
- Celestial-coordinate concepts are hard even for pre-service teachers (27% pre-test). — [SPUCJ 2025](https://so05.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/SPUCJ/article/download/277409/186024/1198072)
- Secondary competency-based curriculum is planned from academic year 2570. — [Banmuang](https://www.banmuang.co.th/news/education/495227)

### Inferences
Indicator density, counting only those LUNARIS can plausibly claim:

| Grade | Science | Technology | Math | Total | Who it reaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ม.3 | ว 3.1 ม.3/2, 3/3, 3/4 (3 core; ม.3/1 gravity as context) | ว 4.2 ม.3/1, 3/2, 3/3 and ว 4.1 ม.3/2, 3/3, 3/4 (6) | ค 2.2 ม.3/2, ค 3.1 ม.3/1 (2) | about 11 | Every student, compulsory basic course |

Why ม.3 is the primary target:
- (a) All students take it.
- (b) The indicators use "สร้างแบบจำลอง", which suits a 3D model with real geometry.
- (c) Within one grade it allows a STEM-integrated unit across science, computing, design and math. That supports the competency story and OBEC's push for integration.
- (d) The age group (14–15) suits the trade-off reasoning of the site-selection task, if it is scaffolded.
- (e) ม.3 sits at the end of lower secondary, where ว 4.2 ม.3/1 asks for "แอปพลิเคชันที่มีการบูรณาการกับวิชาอื่น".

Present ม.6 as an "advanced track". The horizon-coordinate match is the most technically precise alignment LUNARIS has, and is worth highlighting to judges. Primary indicators (ป.4/2 phases, ป.6/1 eclipses, ป.6/2 space technology) can be cited as prior-knowledge links, not as targets.

Caveats for the proposal:
- LUNARIS does not cover tides (part of ม.3/3).
- The Moon phases shown are lit-hemisphere geometry, not the phase as viewed from Earth, unless a "view from Earth" camera exists or is added.
- Claims for "สร้างแบบจำลอง" indicators need student-construction activities alongside the simulation.

### Gaps
- No Thai study was found on how ม.3 students perform specifically on ว 3.1 ม.3/3 (Moon phases) misconceptions. That evidence would strengthen the grade choice; another researcher may cover it.
- The indicator counts in the table are my own mapping judgment, not an official alignment.
