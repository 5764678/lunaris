# Audio: sources and licences

No audio file is rendered by this project or stored in this repository. The cues are in
`audio-cues.json`; the TD mixes from a local copy at assembly.

## Voices

The students' own voices only, recorded by the team (`vo-A.m4a`, `vo-B.m4a`, and the on-camera
clips). **No AI voice and no text-to-speech**, not even as a temporary track.

## Music

| Use | Track | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| One quiet ambient or piano bed, 18–26 dB under the voices | **To be supplied by the team** | record the URL here | must be a written free licence; record it here |

None of the bundled video-shotcraft tracks is used: `cat-walk`, `house-vibez` and `tonight-hiphop`
are beat-driven and wrong for this film (TD decision, 22 Nov 2026), `bgm-tech-house` has no
recorded URL, and `g-eazy-nba-type` is excluded by the brief. If no track arrives, the video
ships with SFX only.
